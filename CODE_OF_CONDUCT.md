# Community Code of Conduct

REPNet Community Edition welcomes technical criticism, replication attempts, alternative models, negative results, and careful disagreement.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attack people;
- distinguish tested results from hypotheses and speculation;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal abuse;
- respect privacy and do not post personal or confidential data;
- keep technical discussions reproducible and specific where possible;
- accept that a failed experiment or falsified mechanism is a useful research result.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect constructive collaboration.

Questions about project scope should be handled through issues or discussions focused on the relevant technical point.

# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-07

First public **REPNet Community Edition** release.

### Included

- cleaned, installable NumPy reference implementation;
- deterministic weighted graph primitives;
- dissipative regulatory dynamics consistent with the closure-energy formulation;
- closure-energy and flux observables;
- external forcing hook;
- reproducible example;
- regression tests for dissipative relaxation;
- public architecture and concept documentation;
- explicit separation between Community Edition and private research material;
- MIT license and citation metadata.

### Not included

Historical persistent memory, private research artifacts, local orchestration, experimental LLM adapters, unreleased readout experiments, and machine-specific state are intentionally excluded from Community Edition v1.0.
